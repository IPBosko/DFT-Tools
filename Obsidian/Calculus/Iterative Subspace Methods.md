Iterative subspace methods consist of keeping in memory all (or some of) the [[Density Matrix|density matrices]] computed at the previous steps and in choosing the updated density matrix in the [[Vector Subspace|vector subspace]] generated by all the density matrices stored in memory, in such a way that some criterion is minimized [1].
