The key notion of PL is a proposition ($p$). A proposition $p$ is a variable that can take the values `true` or `false` and no others. 

PL deals with a complete proposition and decides whether its true or false. It does not deal with a complex proposition. Atomic, elementary notion of propositions.

One can build new propositions from given ones with the help of [[Logical Operators]]. A combination of two propositions unified via a binary logical operator is another proposition.
