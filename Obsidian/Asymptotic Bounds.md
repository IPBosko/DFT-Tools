There are three mathematical metrics used to relate the growth of one function relative to the growth of a simpler one: 
1) **Big $\mathbf{O}$**
   **Definition**: $f(x)\in \mathbf{O}(g(x))$ if there exist $c,x_0>0$ such that $f(x)\leq c\cdot g(x)$ for all $x\geq x_0$.
2) **Big $\mathbf{\Omega}$**
   **Definition**: $f(x)\in \mathbf{\Omega}(g(x))$ if there exist $c,x_0>0$ such that $f(x)\geq c\cdot g(x)$ for all $x\geq x_0$.
3) **Big** $\mathbf{\Theta}$
   **Definition**: $f(x)\in \mathbf{\Theta}(g(x))$ if there exist $c_1,c_2,x_0>0$ such that $c_1\cdot g(x)\geq f(x)\geq c_2\cdot g(x)$ for all $x\geq x_0$.
