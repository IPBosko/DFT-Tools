Density functional theory (DFT) is a method of solving the [[Schrödinger Equation]]. The theory is exact in principle and requires approximations in practice. The most popular practical DFT method is [[Kohn–Sham DFT]].

## Applications

The theory is widely used as a quantum mechanical modelling tool in chemistry, physics, and material design to study the electronic structure of atoms, molecules, and condensed phases.
