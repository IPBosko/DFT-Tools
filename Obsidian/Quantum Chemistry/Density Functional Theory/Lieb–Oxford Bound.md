Is an inequality used to constrain exchange functionals [1] (part of the [[Exchange-Correlation Energy]]), e.g. [[PW91]], [[PBE]], and [[VMT]].

The form of the inequality is $$\frac{E_{\text{xc}}[\rho]}{E^{\text{LDA}}_{\text{x}}[\rho]}\leq\lambda_{\text{LO}}.$$Here, $E^{\text{LDA}}_{\text{x}}[\rho]$ is the [[Local Density Approximation]] exchange energy. The conventional value of the constant is $\lambda_{\text{LO}}=2.273$ [2, 3], or the slightly tighter Chan–Handy value $\lambda_{\text{LO}}=2.215$ [4].
