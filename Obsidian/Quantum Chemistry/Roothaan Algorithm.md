The Roothaan Algorithm is the simplest [[Fixed Point]] procedure associated with the nonlinear [[Eigenvalue Problem]] of the [[Hartree–Fock]] equations: $$F(D)C = SCE,\quad D = CC^*,\quad C^*SC=I_N$$where $F(D)$ denotes the [[Fock Matrix]]: $$F(D)=h+G(D),$$$I_N$ is the [[Identity Matrix]] of rank $N$, $E$ is an $N\times N$ [[Hermitian Matrix]], $C$ is the $N\times N$ [[Matrix of MO Coefficients]], $S$ is the [[Overlap Matrix]], and $D$ is the [[Density Matrix]] [1].
