# What is it all about?

Here we start our practical journey into density functional theory (DFT)!

## What is DFT?

DFT is a method of solving the Schrödinger equation for molecules.

## Schrödinger equation? Molecules?

The solution of the Schrödinger equation contains all the information about the system it describes. 

Information about molecules is needed by chemists, physicists, pharmacologists, materials scientists, and others.

## Why DFT?

DFT is exact in principle, and requires approximations in practice. It has a rich history of applications and a great community.
